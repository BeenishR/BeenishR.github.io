My Journey & Perspective

My career began on the front lines of global trade in the Pakistan Customs Service, where I spent over eight years leading large-scale operations, implementing trade policy, managing airport logistics, and running complex procurement initiatives. Managing border compliance and trade flows gave me a deep, realistic understanding of how global commerce operates under high-stakes pressures.

However, seeing the massive reach of global supply chains drove me to explore how we can make them both resilient and environmentally accountable. That ambition led me to Columbia University’s M.S. in Sustainability Management, where I’ve focused on Scope 3 emissions modeling, net-zero strategy, and climate disclosure compliance.

I thrive at the intersection of regulatory compliance, vendor procurement, and sustainability—helping organizations build resilient supply chains that meet both bottom-line targets and net-zero commitments.
