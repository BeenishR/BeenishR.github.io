My Journey & Perspective

My career began as a pharmacist validating cold chain inventory for a little known pharmaceutical company in Pakistan; progressing into working on the front lines of international trade. I spent over eight years leading large-scale operations, implementing trade policy, managing logistics operations at ports, and running time sensitive procurement operations. Managing border compliance and trade flows gave me a deep, realistic understanding of how global commerce operates under high-stakes pressures.

However, seeing the massive reach of global supply chains drove me to explore how we can make them both resilient and environmentally accountable. That ambition led me to Columbia University’s M.S. in Sustainability Management, where I’ve focused on developing expertise on Supply Chain Emissions modeling, developing Net-Zero Strategy, building technical skills on reporting framework and climate risk analysis and mitigation.

I thrive at the intersection of stakeholder engagement, regulatory compliance, and sustainable operations; helping organizations build resilient supply chains of the future.
